# Pattern catalogue

Based on Wikipedia:Signs of AI writing (WikiProject AI Cleanup), kept from
humanizer v2 and trimmed. Each entry: what to look for, then a before/after.

## Content

### 1. Inflated significance

Watch: stands as, serves as, is a testament to, plays a crucial/pivotal role,
underscores its importance, reflects broader, symbolizing its enduring, marking a
shift, key turning point, evolving landscape, indelible mark, deeply rooted.

Before: The institute's 1989 founding marked a pivotal moment in the evolution of
regional statistics, part of a broader movement toward decentralization.
After: The institute was founded in 1989 to publish regional statistics
separately from the national office.

### 2. Notability padding

Watch: independent coverage, national media outlets, written by a leading expert,
active social media presence, has been featured in.

Before: Her views have been cited in the NYT, BBC, FT and The Hindu, and she has
over 500,000 followers.
After: In a 2024 NYT interview she argued AI regulation should target outcomes,
not methods.

### 3. Participle padding

Tacked-on -ing clauses that add no information: highlighting, underscoring,
ensuring, reflecting, contributing to, fostering, showcasing, encompassing.

Before: The palette of blue, green and gold resonates with the region, symbolizing
bluebonnets and the Gulf, reflecting a deep connection to the land.
After: The building uses blue, green and gold. The architect chose them to
reference local bluebonnets and the Gulf coast.

### 4. Promotional tone

Watch: boasts, vibrant, rich (figurative), profound, nestled, in the heart of,
breathtaking, must-visit, stunning, renowned, commitment to.

Before: Nestled in the breathtaking Gonder region, Alamata Raya Kobo stands as a
vibrant town with rich heritage and stunning natural beauty.
After: Alamata Raya Kobo is a town in Gonder, Ethiopia, known for its weekly
market and an 18th-century church.

### 5. Vague attribution

Watch: experts argue, observers note, industry reports, some critics say,
several studies suggest, it is widely believed.

Before: Experts believe the river plays a crucial role in the regional ecosystem.
After: The river supports several endemic fish species, per a 2019 Chinese
Academy of Sciences survey.

### 6. Formulaic "challenges" sections

Watch: Despite its X, it faces several challenges. Despite these challenges...
Challenges and Future Prospects. Looking ahead.

Before: Despite prosperity, Korattur faces challenges typical of urban areas.
Despite these challenges, it continues to thrive.
After: Congestion rose after three IT parks opened in 2015. A stormwater drainage
project started in 2022.

## Language

### 7. AI vocabulary

See `excess-words.md`. Short high-signal set: delve, showcase, underscore,
intricate, meticulous, pivotal, crucial, seamless, robust, comprehensive,
foster, leverage, realm, tapestry, testament, interplay, landscape (abstract),
additionally, moreover, furthermore.

### 8. Copula avoidance

Watch: serves as, stands as, represents, functions as, boasts, features, offers.

Before: Gallery 825 serves as LAAA's exhibition space and boasts over 3,000
square feet.
After: Gallery 825 is LAAA's exhibition space. It has 3,000 square feet.

### 9. Negative parallelism

Watch: not just X, it's Y / not merely / it isn't about X, it's about Y.

Before: It's not merely a song, it's a statement.
After: The beat carries the aggression.

### 10. Rule of three

Groups of three forced for rhythm, especially adjective triplets and
"innovation, inspiration and insight" style endings.

Before: The event features keynotes, panels and networking. Expect innovation,
inspiration and industry insight.
After: The event has talks and panels, with time to mingle between them.

### 11. Synonym cycling

The protagonist / the main character / the central figure / the hero, all in one
paragraph. Pick one name and repeat it. Repetition is human.

### 12. False ranges

"From X to Y" where X and Y are not endpoints of any scale.

Before: from the singularity of the Big Bang to the grand cosmic web, from the
birth of stars to the dance of dark matter.
After: The book covers the Big Bang, star formation and dark matter.

### 13. Over-hedging and under-hedging

Both are tells. "It could potentially possibly be argued that X might have some
effect" -> "X may affect Y." But zero hedging across 1,000 words of contested
claims is equally artificial. Match confidence to actual knowledge.

## Surface

### 14. Em dashes

More than one per 300 words is a flag. Replace with commas, full stops or
parentheses. Keep the ones doing real interruption work.

### 15. Mechanical boldface

Bolding key terms on first mention throughout. Bold sparingly or not at all.

### 16. Inline-header lists

`- **Performance:** Performance was improved...` Collapse into prose or use plain
bullets that carry information rather than restating the label.

### 17. Title Case Headings

Use sentence case unless a style guide says otherwise.

### 18. Emojis as section markers

Remove in professional and reference writing.

### 19. Curly quotes and stray Unicode

Normalise to straight quotes when the target expects plain text.

## Chat residue

### 20. Assistant artifacts

Of course! Certainly! I hope this helps. Let me know if you'd like me to expand.
Here is a... Great question. Delete all of it.

### 21. Knowledge-cutoff hedges

As of my last update, while specific details are limited, based on available
information. Either find the fact or drop the sentence.

### 22. Sycophancy

You're absolutely right, that's an excellent point. Cut.

## Filler

### 23. Wordy constructions

- in order to -> to
- due to the fact that -> because
- at this point in time -> now
- in the event that -> if
- has the ability to -> can
- it is important to note that -> (delete)
- a wide range of / a variety of -> the actual number or examples

### 24. Uplift endings

The future looks bright. Exciting times ahead. A step in the right direction.
As the field continues to evolve. End on a fact or stop.
