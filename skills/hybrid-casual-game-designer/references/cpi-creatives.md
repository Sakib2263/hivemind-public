# CPI Creative Brief Protocol
## reference: cpi-creatives.md

---

## VIDEO REQUIREMENTS

### Supersonic: 6 creative videos
1. Satisfying gameplay loop
2. Mechanic focus (close-up of primary action)
3. Fail bait (near-fail → clutch save)
4. Chaos to order narrative
5. Level showcase (impressive board layout)
6. Sara moment (cozy theme highlight)

### Voodoo: 5 × 30-second videos
1. Problem-solving (satisfying puzzle solution)
2. Emotional relief (stress-relief framing)
3. Funny fail (relatable failure)
4. Impossible puzzle (challenge the viewer)
5. Art style showcase

---

## 15-SECOND AD BRIEF FORMAT

```
CREATIVE [N] — [HOOK TYPE]
Hook: [Fail Bait / Satisfying Loop / Chaos-to-Order / Challenge / Sara Moment]

0–3s HOOK:
  Scene: [exact visual]
  Goal: Stop the scroll

3–10s GAMEPLAY:
  [What mechanic action is shown?]
  [What tension/satisfaction moment happens?]

10–14s PAYOFF:
  [Resolution — win state or reveal]

14–15s CTA:
  Text: "[Install prompt]"

THUMBNAIL BRIEF:
  Composition: [thirds description]
  Headline (5 words max): "[TEXT]"
  
SARA TEST: Would this make a 38-year-old woman tap? [YES/NO/WHY]
```

---

## HOOK TYPES

**Fail Bait:** Tray at 4/5 slots → player saves it. Universal appeal.
**Satisfying Loop:** Core mechanic relief moment on repeat. Best for high-animation games.
**Chaos to Order:** Before (messy) → After (clean). Peak Sara motivation.
**Challenge:** "Can you solve this?" Hard-looking board. Drives high-engagement installs.
**Sara Moment:** Purely aesthetic. Cozy, warm, tactile close-up. Best D30 targeting.
